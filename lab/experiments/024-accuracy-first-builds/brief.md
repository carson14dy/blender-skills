# Brief: garden shed

Written the way a user would ask. Two values are left out on purpose (the roof pitch and the door size), to test how the build handles gaps.

> Build me a garden shed in Blender. It's 10 ft wide and 12 ft deep, and the walls are 8 ft tall, with a gable roof. It sits on a concrete pad.
>
> Four windows, 2 ft wide and 3 ft tall, two on each long side, and a door in the middle of the front.
>
> Lap siding on the walls, painted sage green, with white trim around the windows and the door. Asphalt shingles on the roof.
>
> Put the wall studs in at 16 in. on center: I want to show the framing in a cutaway later.
>
> Render one hero image at 1920x1080 from a front corner, in daylight.

## Gaps (on purpose)

1. **Roof pitch**: not given. It changes what the user sees (ridge height, roof area), so the procedure says ask.
2. **Door size**: not given. A standard size exists (3 ft 0 in. by 6 ft 8 in.), so the procedure says look it up instead of asking.
